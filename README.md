# Study of a Late Qing Chinese Manuscript (5 Volumes)

**About the Project**
This repository contains the results of a textological, paleographic, and codicological analysis of a five-volume Chinese manuscript dated 1721 (Kangxi era). The scans of the manuscript are published on Zenodo (see "Sources"). Here we present translations, decipherments, methodology, and Python scripts for working with the database.

**Subject of Study**
A five-volume collection of prophetic texts, including:
*   Methodology of decipherment (Volume 1).
*   "Song of the Prediction of Heaven and Earth" (乾坤預知歌).
*   "Song of the Baked Cake" (燒餅歌).
*   "Poems of Chan Master Huangbo" (黃蘗禪師詩).
*   "Stele of Zhuge Liang" (孔明碑記).
*   "Heaven's Secret Mystery" (透天玄機).
*   Reference tables (I Ching, 60-year cycle, chronology).

**Key Findings**
1.  **Paleographic Analysis:** At least **4–5 copyists** were identified, working at different times (18th–20th centuries). Different ink compositions, handwriting styles, and blank pages confirm the "living" nature of the document.
2.  **Dating:** The final edition was made **after 1905** (mention of the Russo-Japanese War and telegraph).
3.  **Riddles:** A map of **39 solved riddles** has been compiled (23 are well-known, 15 were deciphered by us). Three riddles remain unsolved.
4.  **Scripts:** The `/scripts/` folder contains Python scripts (in Russian) for updating the database.

**Repository Structure**

*   `/docs/ru/riddles_analysis.md` — analysis of 15 riddles (in Russian).
