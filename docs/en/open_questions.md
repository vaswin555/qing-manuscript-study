# Open Questions: Riddles Requiring Further Investigation

This document contains a list of riddles and hidden codes from the five-volume manuscript that are **not yet solved** or **remain uncertain**. 
We publish them as material for further research to draw attention to these "blank spots" and not lose valuable observations.

---

## 1. Complex Structural Riddles (Unsolved)

These radical combinations could not be unambiguously matched to a specific character or name.

*   **`牛鴨二八倒柿丁` (Vol. 2, p. 1)**
    *   **Analysis:** 牛 (Ox) + 鴨 (Duck) + 二八 (16) + 倒柿 (Inverted Persimmon) + 丁 (Ding).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Indicates a name, year, or hidden number. Possibly related to the 28 constellations (二八 = 28) and a specific year.
*   **`有一裏腳鬼` (Vol. 3, p. 1)**
    *   **Analysis:** 裏 (Inside) + 腳 (Foot) + 鬼 (Ghost).
    *   **Hypothesis:** "A ghost with a foot inside." Possibly a visual riddle for a character we could not find.
*   **`兩個人頭生一草` (Vol. 3, p. 5)**
    *   **Analysis:** 艹 (Grass) + 人 (Person) + 人 (Person).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Possibly `茶` (Tea) or `英` (Hero). Requires verification based on context.
*   **`二人斟命在樹頭` (Vol. 3, p. 2)**
    *   **Analysis:** 二 (Two) + 人 (Person) + 木 (Wood).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Possibly `來` (To come) or `李` (Li). Context is unclear.
*   **`金斗二四加一五` (Vol. 2, p. 15)**
    *   **Analysis:** Golden Dipper: 2-4 + 1-5.
    *   **Hypothesis:** Mathematical riddle. 24 + 15 = 39? Or 2+4+1+5 = 12? Requires verification.
*   **`眠木` (Vol. 1, p. 10)**
    *   **Analysis:** 眠 (Sleep) + 木 (Wood).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Possibly `相` (Xiang — minister) or a surname. Not proven.
*   **`無頭普` (Vol. 1, p. 6)**
    *   **Analysis:** 普 without 日 (top).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Possibly `亞` (Asia, second). Not proven.
*   **`劍上生兔` (Vol. 1, p. 6)**
    *   **Analysis:** 劍 (Sword) + 兔 (Rabbit).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Indication of the Year of the Rabbit, but how does it relate to the sword? Unclear.

## 2. "Error-Distortions" Requiring Verification (Vol. 4 & 5)

We have identified several trigram substitutions. Some are already proven (Gou, Tong Ren), but others require further verification.

*   **`離上坎下` in the `山水蒙` hexagram (Vol. 4, p. 10)**
    *   **In the text:** Li (Fire) above, Kan (Water) below.
    *   **Canon:** Should be `艮上坎下` (Gen + Kan).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Substitution of Gen with Li. Requires verification.
*   **`乾上離下` in the `無妄` hexagram (Vol. 4, p. 16)**
    *   **In the text:** Qian (Heaven) above, Li (Fire) below.
    *   **Canon:** Should be `乾上震下` (Qian + Zhen).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Substitution of Zhen with Li. Requires verification.
*   **`艮上艮下` in the `火山賁` hexagram (Vol. 5, p. 2)**
    *   **In the text:** Gen (Mountain) above, Gen (Mountain) below.
    *   **Canon:** Should be `艮上離下` (Gen + Li).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Substitution of Li with Gen. Requires verification.
*   **`巽上艮下` in the `風澤中孚` hexagram (Vol. 5, p. 4)**
    *   **In the text:** Xun (Wind) above, Gen (Mountain) below.
    *   **Canon:** Should be `巽上兌下` (Xun + Dui).
    *   **Hypothesis:** Substitution of Dui with Gen. Requires verification.

## 3. Disputed Interpretations (Hypotheses)

These interpretations seem logical but do not have direct evidence in the text.

*   **`兔頭歲 = 1927`** — The Year of the Rabbit (1927) coincides with the beginning of the Chinese Civil War. But there is no direct link to this event in the text. It may simply be an indication of the Year of the Rabbit.
*   **`至於八月有凶 = 8th month`** — We believe this is a coordinate of catastrophe (linked to the omission in Vol. 3). But this is our interpretation, not a direct quote.
